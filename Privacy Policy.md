# Privacy Policy for Walpy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Walpy is an open-source wallpaper app maintained by Tom in Dresden, Germany.
For privacy questions, contact the maintainer through the
[Walpy GitHub repository](https://github.com/zTomz/Walpy).

## Data Walpy processes

Walpy does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics,
or tracking SDKs. The developer does not operate an app backend and does not
receive or store users' personal data.

To provide image search and wallpaper downloads, Walpy connects directly to the
Pixabay API and Pixabay image servers over HTTPS. These requests may contain:

- the search term entered by the user;
- the selected color or image category;
- technical connection data necessarily transmitted with an internet request,
  such as the IP address, request time, and device or network information.

Pixabay (a Canva Germany GmbH brand) processes data under its own
[Privacy Policy](https://pixabay.com/service/privacy/). Walpy does not control
Pixabay's processing or retention of this data.

## Local storage and device access

Downloaded images are stored locally in Walpy's app-specific storage so the app
can set them as the home-screen or lock-screen wallpaper. Walpy does not upload
these files. App data can be removed through the operating system's app settings
or by uninstalling Walpy. A wallpaper already applied through the operating
system may remain until the user changes it.

## Sharing, sale, and retention

Walpy does not sell personal data. Data is disclosed only to Pixabay as needed
to retrieve search results and image files. The developer does not retain this
request data because Walpy has no developer-operated server. Pixabay's own
retention rules apply to data it receives.

App stores and operating-system providers may independently process data when
users download, install, or use the app. Their respective privacy policies
apply to that processing.

## User rights

Depending on the user's location, privacy laws may provide rights such as
access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or data portability.
Because the developer does not receive or store personal data through Walpy,
requests concerning Pixabay data should also be directed to Pixabay. Users may
contact the maintainer through the repository linked above for questions about
Walpy.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Walpy's functionality or data processing
changes. The current version is published in the Walpy repository.
